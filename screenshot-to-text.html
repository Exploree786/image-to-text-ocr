    How to copy text from a screenshot

    1. Capture the screen: Win+Shift+S on Windows, Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 on a Mac (both copy to the clipboard).
    2. Click on this page and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac). On a phone, choose browse files and pick the screenshot.
    3. Choose the language of the text, then Extract text.
    4. Fix any mistakes in the editor, then copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

    Tips for clean screenshot OCR

    • Zoom in first. Enlarge the page or app before you capture it. Enlarging a small screenshot afterwards adds no detail; aim for letters at least about 20 px tall.
    • Capture only the text. Menus, icons and sidebars can turn into stray characters.
    • Try Enhance on dark themes. If light text on a dark background comes out badly, tick Enhance image and compare.
    • Check code and numbers. Characters such as 0 and O, or 1, l and I, look alike, so review error codes, keys and commands before using them.

    Good uses for screenshot OCR

    Screenshots suit text that can’t be selected: error dialogs, text inside videos, slides in a meeting, app screens and messages. Because the image is read on your own device instead of being uploaded, you can also use it for screens that show private details. Up to 10 screenshots can be added at once, and their text is combined into one result with the file name above each section.

    Common questions

    Can I paste a screenshot directly?

    Yes. Copy the screenshot to your clipboard and press Ctrl+V anywhere on this page. Each pasted image is added to the list.

    Does it work with screenshots from my phone?

    Yes. Choose browse files and select the screenshot from your gallery. Current mobile versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge are supported.

    Will code indentation be kept?

    Line breaks and paragraph gaps are kept where possible, but exact indentation and column alignment may be lost, so check the layout of code after pasting it.

    Are my screenshots uploaded?

    No. They are processed in your browser. Only the OCR engine and language files are downloaded from the jsDelivr CDN.
